Privacy Policy

This is a sample privacy policy page for the demo preview. The final version will be tailored to the live platform.

নোটিশ (খসড়া): এটি ডেমো প্রিভিউয়ের জন্য একটি নমুনা প্রাইভেসি পলিসি। লঞ্চের আগে আইনি পরামর্শ নিয়ে চূড়ান্ত সংস্করণ তৈরি হবে।

1. Information We Collect

When you enroll in a course or contact us, we may collect: your name, email address, phone number, and course selection. Payment details are handled by our payment provider and are never stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process enrollments and provide access to courses
  • To send course updates, certificates and support messages
  • To improve our courses and website experience

3. Cookies

The demo site does not use tracking cookies. The live version will use only essential cookies and analytics with your consent.

4. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. Data is shared only with service providers strictly necessary to run the platform (e.g., payment processing, email delivery).

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us through the contact page.

6. Contact

For any privacy question, reach us via the contact page.
